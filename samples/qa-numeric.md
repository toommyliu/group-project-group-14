- (10 points) How many milliliters are in one liter?
  - **Correct**
    - 1000 units:mL

- (10 points) Estimate the value of pi to two decimal places.
  - **Correct**
    - 3.14 ±0.01

- (10 points) A signal measures between what two voltages? Give the safe range.
  - **Correct**
    - [3.0, 5.0] units:V

- (5 points) The time complexity of binary search is O(log n).
  - **Correct**
    - True

- (5 points) TCP is a connectionless protocol.
  - **Type**
    - True/False
  - **Correct**
    - False
