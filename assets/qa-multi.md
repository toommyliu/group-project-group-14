## Version A

- (10 points) Convert decimal 5 to binary [A].
  - **Correct**
    - 101
- (10 points) Convert decimal 7 to binary [A].
  - **Correct**
    - 111
- (10 points) What is least significant bit (LSB) of the binary number 1010 [A]?
  - **Correct**
    - 0
- (10 points) What is most significant bit (MSB) of the binary number 1010 [A]?
  - **Correct**
    - 1
